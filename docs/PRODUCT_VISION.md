# ThinkStack

## Vision

ThinkStack is a Decision Intelligence Platform that helps users make confident decisions by understanding their needs, comparing alternatives, explaining trade-offs, and recommending the most suitable option.

Instead of simply comparing products, ThinkStack guides users from confusion to confidence.

## Mission

Help users make smarter decisions, even if they have zero prior knowledge about the product or category.

## Target Audience

- Students
- Professionals
- Job Seekers
- First-time Buyers
- Parents
- Tech Enthusiasts
- Small Business Owners

## Core Philosophy

A user should never have to open five different websites before making a decision.

ThinkStack should become their single trusted decision companion.
